## I'm a professional

My approach to decisions is to **act intelligently**; think twice then execute once (succesfully). In my role of managing computer infrastructure, mistakes lead to downtime which increases operational costs. Considering all parameters, while maintaining a steady rate of daily ad-hoc problem solving, requires me to come up with creative solutions that must be coordinated decisively.  
Good decisions need a forwards thinking analytical mindset. Bad decisions ignore facts and cause unexpected work in the future.  
Every day I strive to assume the right mindset for each situation and make only good decisions. I am proud of the low rate of unexpected situations that are (in)directlty caused by my team and myself.

My approach to communication is **be transparent**; be honest and provide complete information. In my role of managing my team mates, obfuscated and missing communication slows down business processes. Communication is a constant stream which cannot be paused or run away from. Keeping track of all ongoing discussions, project plans, and ad-hoc situations requires me to process information correctly and quickly.
Every day I strive to complement missing context, assume the perspective of my communication partner, and engage in discussions with positive mindset. I am proud of the succeeding [inbox-zero strategy](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/inbox_zero#Noun) in tandem with a high colleague-satisfaction score.
